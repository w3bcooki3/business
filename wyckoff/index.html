07

Questions people ask

How long does a new prescription take?

Usually 15 to 20 minutes. If your doctor sent it electronically, text us and it will be ready when you walk in.

Can I pick up someone else’s prescription?

Yes, with their name and date of birth. Some controlled medicines need your ID.

Do you have COVID and flu tests?

Yes, at the counter. Depending on your plan they may be free.

My medicine isn’t covered. What now?

We call your doctor about a covered alternative or a prior authorization, and we tell you the cash price before you decide.
