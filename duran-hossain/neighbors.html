    Vaccinations

    Walk in. Roll up your sleeve. Ten minutes.

    This is the real list — New York sets exactly what a pharmacist may give, and from what age. If it isn’t here, we’re not allowed to, and we’ll tell you who is.

    • Yellow fever, typhoid, rabies: travel clinic — we’ll name one nearby.
    • Bloodwork: your doctor, or a lab like Quest or Labcorp.
    • Kids under 19: most vaccines free at the doctor’s through Vaccines for Children. Flu from 2 and COVID-19 from 3 we can do.

    Insurance

    Before you come in, check your pocket.

    Since April 2023, New York Medicaid prescriptions run through a state program called NYRx — not your plan. It’s the number one reason a card “didn’t work” at a chain.

    Who runs it

    “My mother waited eleven years for somebody at a counter to explain her prescriptions in Spanish. That’s the whole reason this place exists.”

    — Luz Durán, PharmD, co-owner with Nasir Hossain, PharmD

    1. 2007

      Luz and Nasir meet in pharmacy school at St. John’s.

    2. 2009

      A failing storefront under the 7 train becomes ours. Luz’s mom does the books.

    3. 2012

      One bicycle, one cooler bag: free delivery begins.

    4. 2016

      The counter now speaks nine languages.

    5. 2023

      NYRx arrives. We spend spring sorting out neighbors’ cards.

    6. Today

      Still one shop. On purpose.

    Who to call, and when

    We’re a pharmacy, not an emergency room. If someone is in danger, don’t call us first. Call 911.

    • Emergency911

      Chest pain, trouble breathing, a severe allergic reaction, an overdose, or someone who won’t wake up.

      Call 911
    • PoisoningPoison Control

      Took the wrong medicine or a double dose? Free and confidential, 24 hours a day.

      1-800-222-1222
    • Crisis988 Lifeline

      Suicide and mental-health crisis support. Call or text any time, in English or Spanish.

      Call or text 988
