    A

    Medication review

    Thirty minutes with a pharmacist and every bottle you own — including the one your cousin gave you. A written summary goes to your doctor. Free for most Medicare Part D members.

    B

    Blister packs

    Your month sealed into bubbles marked morning, noon, evening and bed. No extra charge. Usually asked for after a hospital discharge with nine new bottles.

    C

    Compounding

    The cream at a strength nobody makes, the liquid for someone who can’t swallow tablets, the dye-free version. Non-sterile only — we refer the rest.

    D

    Diabetes care

    Meters, strips, monitors — and somebody who sits down and shows you what the numbers mean. Insulin goes out in a cold pack.

    E

    Fitted equipment

    Compression stockings and braces measured in store, coverage checked before you pay. Walkers, commodes, ostomy supplies in stock.

    06 Free delivery

    Order by three. At your door by eight.

    No minimum, no fee, no tip expected. Our own staff ride the bags out — so whoever rings your bell can answer a question about what’s inside. Insulin travels cold. Controlled medicines need an ID and a signature.

    Same-day covers Jackson Heights, Elmhurst, Corona, East Elmhurst and Woodside.

      08 Insurance
