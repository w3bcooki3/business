Questions we hear a lot.

Something else? Call (212) 555-0148 and a pharmacist will answer.

Do you deliver?

Yes. Same-day delivery anywhere in Manhattan below 96th Street, and delivery within about two hours in Murray Hill, Kips Bay, Gramercy and Rose Hill. Refrigerated medicines go out cold-packed.

Can I transfer my prescriptions to you?

Yes. Call us with your old pharmacy’s name and location. We contact them, move your prescriptions and refills over, and tell you when your first fill is ready.

Do you take my insurance?

We accept most plans in New York, including Medicare Part D, NY Medicaid (NYRx), Fidelis Care, Healthfirst, EmblemHealth and the major employer plans. Check the plan list, or call us.

Do I need an appointment for a flu shot?

No. Walk in any time we’re open. For travel vaccines or a group visit, call ahead so we can make sure we have it in stock.

How long does a new prescription take?

Most are ready the same day, often within the hour. Specialty and fertility medicines may need to be ordered in, or need your insurer’s approval first. We’ll tell you the timing up front.

Is there discount pricing if I’m uninsured?

Yes. Ask before you pay. We check discount prices and manufacturer savings programs for you.

Can you put my medicines in pill packs?

Yes. We sort your regular medicines into sealed packs labelled by day and time, and refill them on schedule. Ask at the counter to set it up.

In an emergency, don’t wait for us.

Hospital discharge

Going home from NYU Langone, Bellevue or another hospital? We fill your discharge medicines while you pack.

What we do

  • Have it ready to pick up on your way out, or at your door the same day.
  • Handle prior authorization with your doctor and insurer.
  • Call your care team when a dose, form or quantity needs checking.
  • Order specialty medicines in, often by the next business day.
  • Walkers, canes, shower chairs and wound-care supplies for the first weeks home.

What to bring

  • Your discharge paperwork or medication list.
  • Your insurance card.
  • Ask your care team to send prescriptions to Meridian Pharmacy, 488 Second Ave, New York 10016.

Fertility & IVF

We order your fertility medicines and deliver them on your clinic’s timeline, kept cold all the way. Ask for Kevin Zhou.

What we do

  • Refrigerated medicines delivered in insulated, cold-packed boxes.
  • Delivery timed to your cycle start, including weekends.
  • Needles, swabs and sharps containers included with your order.
  • We coordinate directly with your fertility clinic.

What to bring

  • Your clinic’s name and the date your cycle starts.
  • Your insurance or fertility benefit card.
  • A delivery window when someone can put the box in the fridge.

PrEP, PEP & sexual health

Discreet, judgement-free and private. Ask for the pharmacist; you don’t have to explain at the register.

  • PrEP prescriptions, refills and help with insurance and copay programs.
  • PEP is time-sensitive: call first and we’ll tell you right away when it can be ready.
