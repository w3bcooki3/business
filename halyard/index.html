03 — Maren & the boats

A Montauk kid who got tired of shipping the good fish to Manhattan.

Maren Doyle grew up mending nets on her uncle’s dragger, then spent twelve years cooking in city kitchens that bought from the same docks she grew up on — two days and three middlemen later.

Halyard is the shortcut. Four boats call her by 9 am with what they have; a refrigerated van leaves Montauk at noon and backs up to Conover Street by four. Whatever doesn’t sell becomes tomorrow’s chowder, stock, or staff meal. Nothing goes to the dumpster.

“I don’t write a menu. I answer the phone.”— Maren Doyle, chef & owner

05 — Getting here

Red Hook is not on the subway. That’s the point.

By ferry

NYC Ferry, South Brooklyn route to Red Hook / Atlantic Basin. Eight-minute walk down Conover.

By bus

B61 to Van Brunt & Coffey St, or the B57 from Court Street.

By bike

Citi Bike dock at Reed & Conover. We have racks out front.

By car

Street parking is easy before 7. After that, good luck and godspeed.
