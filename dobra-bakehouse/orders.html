Sunday bread class

Bake with Kasia, once a month

First Sunday of the month, 5pm to 8pm, after the shop closes. Eight people around the bench: mixing, shaping and scoring a country loaf, then a demonstration bake in the deck oven. You go home with your dough, a jar of Babcia’s starter and instructions to keep it alive.

  • $95 per person, includes supper of bread, soup and pickles
  • Next dates: November 1, December 6, January 3
  • No experience needed; ages 14 and up
