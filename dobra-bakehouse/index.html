1974

Babcia, the starter that came over in a jam jar.

“My grandmother fed it every night after dinner, like a cat. When she gave it to me she said, don’t let it go hungry, and don’t let it go to anyone lazy.”

Kasia Nowak, baker and co-founder

Kasia’s grandmother started the rye sour in her kitchen on Nassau Avenue in 1974, with flour from the Polish shop on the corner. It lived in her fridge for forty years, went to college with Kasia, and in 2017 moved into a much bigger tub behind our counter. Every loaf of rye we sell still starts with it.
