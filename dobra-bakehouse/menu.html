The menu, top to bottom.

    Allergens, honestly

    We bake in one small room with wheat, rye, dairy, eggs, sesame, poppy seed and walnuts. We label every item, but we can’t promise anything is free of traces.

    All of our breads except challah are vegan. Nothing here is gluten-free. If you have a severe allergy, call before you come and we’ll talk you through it: (718) 555-0151.
