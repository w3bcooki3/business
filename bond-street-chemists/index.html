The pharmacy

Everything a full-service pharmacy does, with the price told to you before we fill.

  • Prescriptions

    New, refills and transfers. Most insurance accepted. If the price is high, we look for a better one.

    05

    Shelf notes

    The cards our pharmacists pin to the shelves. Honest answers, even when the answer is “don’t buy this.”

    • ColdsSkip the phenylephrine pills.

      In 2023 an FDA advisory panel concluded oral phenylephrine doesn’t work as a decongestant. Ask us what does.

    • SummerOral rehydration salts.

      The thing we hand people most often in August, and before long flights. Better than sports drinks for real dehydration.

    • SunSPF 30 or higher, reapplied.

      Broad-spectrum, every two hours outdoors and after swimming. The best sunscreen is the one you’ll actually reapply.

    • SleepMore melatonin isn’t better.

      Lower doses often work as well. Ask us first if you take blood thinners or other sleep aids.

    06

    Questions

    Can you check whether two of my medicines interact?

    Yes, at the counter and free. Bring the bottles, or photos of the labels, supplements included.

    Do I need a prescription for travel vaccines?

    In New York, pharmacists can give several vaccines without one. For others we coordinate with your doctor. Come in four to six weeks before you fly if you can.

    Are consultations covered by insurance?

    Usually not. Medication reviews are free for anyone filling with us, and we tell you about any fee before booking.

    How fast is a transfer?

    Usually within a day. We call your old pharmacy; you don’t have to.
