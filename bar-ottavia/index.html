    Named for a grandmother who fed the whole block.

    Ottavia Ferrante came to Union Street from Tropea in 1962 with a pasta board, a rolling pin as long as her arm, and strong opinions about garlic. Her grandchildren Ottavia (yes, same name) and Nico grew up under that board. They opened this place to keep feeding the neighborhood the way she did — too much, and with commentary.

    1. 1962

      Nonna Ottavia arrives from Tropea, Calabria. Moves into a third-floor walk-up on Union Street.

    2. 1979

      Her Sunday table regularly seats nineteen. Neighbors start bringing their own chairs.

    3. 2016

      Ottavia & Nico sign a lease on a former hardware store at Court & 1st Place. They keep the tin ceiling.

    4. 2019

      The back garden opens with string lights and a fig tree from a cutting off Nonna’s.

    5. Today

      Same pasta board. Three cooks rolling from 9 am. The rolling pin hangs above the pass.

    The garden, for your people.

    Birthdays, rehearsal dinners, the “we finally all got a sitter” night. The back garden seats 30 under the fig tree, family-style. Heated and tented from November.
