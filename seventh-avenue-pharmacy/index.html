Kids’ medicine, made easier

Getting it in is half the battle.

Flavoring for liquid medicine

Many liquid prescriptions can be flavored at the counter. Ask the pharmacist whether yours can, and which flavors we have that day — your kid usually gets the final vote.

Ask when you drop off, or mention it when you text a refill.

Dose syringes & measuring help

Kitchen spoons aren’t measuring tools. We’ll hand you an oral syringe or dosing cup marked in mL and show you how to read it — line the top edge of the plunger up with the mark.

The amount always comes from the label or your pediatrician. If anything’s unclear, ask us before the first dose.

Pharmacist-led

Ask us before you buy.

Standing in the aisle with two boxes that look the same? That’s what the pharmacy counter is for. No appointment, no charge to ask.

  1. “Is this the infant or the children’s version — and does it matter?”
  2. “Can she take this with the prescription she’s already on?”
  3. “Which thermometer makes sense for a newborn?”
  4. “Is this a pharmacy question or a call-the-pediatrician question?”
  5. “What can I take while I’m breastfeeding?”

Vaccines

Shots for grown-ups, and some for the kids.

Flu season, a booster before the new baby arrives, a catch-up shot for a grandparent. Call ahead so we can check what we have that day.

Ask which ages we can vaccinate.

Who a pharmacist can vaccinate depends on the vaccine and the patient’s age, and the rules change. Tell us who it’s for and we’ll tell you straight away — or point you to your pediatrician.

Refills

Refill by text. One thumb.

  1. Find the Rx number on your bottle’s label.
  2. Text it to (718) 555-0187 — the button below starts the message for you.
  3. We text back when it’s ready, or if we need to call your doctor.
Text a refill Call it in

Prefer not to text health details? Call us, or bring the bottle to the counter. This website never asks for your prescription or personal information.
