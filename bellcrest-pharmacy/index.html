Looking after a parent’s medicines? We’ll work with you.

Many of the people we fill for have a daughter, a son or a neighbour keeping an eye on things. Once your parent tells us it’s okay, you can do almost everything for them.

  1. Your parent tells us who you are

    She can call us, or stop by the counter with you once. We add your name and number to her profile.

  2. We keep you in the loop

    We text you when refills are ready, call before each month of pill packs, and give you her current medicine list for doctor visits.

  3. You pick up, or we deliver

    Pick up with your own photo ID, or set up free delivery to her door.

Live far away? We can set up a three-way call with you, your parent and the pharmacist, in English, Korean, Mandarin or Greek.

Free help choosing a Medicare drug plan

October 15 to December 7

Medicare open enrollment runs every fall.

Plans change their prices and the drugs they cover every year. A plan that suited you last year may not suit you now. Sit down with one of our pharmacists for 30 minutes and compare plans side by side, using your own medicines.

  • Bring your Medicare card and your medicine list, or the bottles
  • We compare together. You decide; we don’t sell plans

    Questions people ask us

    Something else? Call (718) 555‑0147. A real person answers.

    Do you deliver, and does it cost anything?

    Yes, and it’s free. We deliver to Bayside, Douglaston, Little Neck and Oakland Gardens. Call or text to set it up, and tell us if you’d like the same day each month.

    How long does a new prescription take?

    Most are ready the same day. If we need to order a medicine or check with your insurance, we’ll call or text you with a time.

    Can my daughter or son pick up for me?

    Yes. Tell us their name once, by phone or at the counter, and they can pick up with their own photo ID. See help for caregivers.

    Do I need an appointment for a flu shot?

    No. Flu and COVID-19 shots are walk-in during pharmacy hours. Bring your insurance or Medicare card.

    What if I don’t have insurance?

    Ask about our discount prices before we fill. We’ll also look for manufacturer coupons and the lowest-cost option for you.

    Are pill packs really free?

    Yes. Packing and refill sync cost nothing extra. You pay only your usual copays for the medicines inside.
