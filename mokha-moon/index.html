A short explainer

Qishr isn’t coffee. Exactly.

Both come from the same coffee cherry. Coffee is the seed inside, roasted. Qishr — قشر, literally “husk” — is the dried fruit around it, simmered with ginger and cardamom into something closer to a spiced tea.
