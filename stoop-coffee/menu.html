FromMireille'soven

Baked in the back every morning from recipes Mireille learned in her grandmother's kitchen in Jacmel, Haiti, and adjusted for a Brooklyn oven. When the case is empty, it's empty.

  • Pain patate slice$5

    Mireille's grandmother's Haitian sweet-potato pudding cake: banana, coconut milk, evaporated milk, nutmeg, a splash of rum. Dense, custardy, best warm. Allergens: D, E, G

  • Coconut-lime loaf$4.50

    Toasted coconut and lime zest, glazed while warm. Dairy-free. Allergens: E, G, C

  • Banana-rum bread$4.50

    Very ripe bananas, Barbancourt rum, brown sugar crust. The rum bakes out; the flavor doesn't. Allergens: D, E, G, N

  • Bacon, egg & cheese on a roll$8

    Two eggs, thick bacon, American, salt-pepper-ketchup on a Kaiser from the bakery on Lewis Ave. It's New York. Allergens: D, E, G

  • Avocado-pikliz toast$11

    Smashed avocado on toasted sourdough, topped with pikliz, the spicy Haitian slaw of cabbage, carrot and Scotch bonnet. Add an egg for $2. Allergens: G

Allergens

Our kitchen is the size of a closet, so everything shares space with wheat, dairy, eggs and nuts. If you have a serious allergy, ask at the window and we'll tell you exactly what's in it, or tell you honestly to skip it.
