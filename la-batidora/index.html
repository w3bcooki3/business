La de la casa

Morir Soñando

“To die dreaming.”

Fresh orange juice poured slowly into cold evaporated milk with sugar and lots of ice. Pour too fast and it curdles, so Yanelis pours every one herself. It tastes like a creamsicle that grew up in Santo Domingo.

La familia

Doña Mireya opened La Batidora in 1997 with one blender and a cooler of fruit from the market on Dyckman. Her daughter Yanelis runs the counter now. Mireya still comes in to check the zapote is ripe enough. It usually isn’t, by her standards.
