二

帶草藥來核對Bring your herbs

Bring your herbs. We check them against your prescriptions.帶你的草藥來，我們幫你核對處方藥。

Many people take Chinese herbs, herbal teas or supplements along with their prescriptions. Some combinations can change how a medicine works.很多人服用處方藥時，也會服用中藥、涼茶或保健品。有些配搭會影響藥效。

Bring them in. A pharmacist reads every label with you and checks each one for interactions with your prescriptions — in your language.請把它們帶來。藥劑師會和你一起看清每個標籤，逐一核對與處方藥有沒有相互作用，並用你的語言解釋。

What we do, and what we don’t我們做甚麼，不做甚麼

We check for interactions and safety. We do not say whether an herb treats an illness — please ask your doctor about that. Don’t stop a prescription on your own; talk to us or your doctor first.我們檢查相互作用及安全問題。我們不會說某種草藥能否治病，這方面請問你的醫生。請不要自行停藥，先和我們或你的醫生商量。

Put these in one bag把這些放進一個袋

  • Herb packets, or the herbalist’s list of ingredients中藥包，或中醫師開的藥方
  • Herbal teas and soups you drink often經常飲用的涼茶或湯水
  • Vitamins, supplements and powders維他命、保健品及沖劑
  • Medicated oils, plasters and creams藥油、藥膏及膏布
  • All your prescription bottles所有處方藥瓶
  1. 1Sit down with a pharmacist.和藥劑師坐下來。
  2. 2We check each item against your prescriptions.逐一核對你的處方藥。
  3. 3If something needs care, we write it down for you and your doctor.如有需要注意的地方，我們會寫下來，給你和你的醫生參考。
三

藥物檢查及標籤Reviews & labels

Know what you take. Read what you take.清楚你吃甚麼藥，看得清楚標籤。

Medication review藥物檢查

Bring every bottle: prescriptions, pharmacy medicines and vitamins. We go through them one by one — what each is for, when to take it, and whether any two do the same job.請帶齊所有藥物，包括處方藥、成藥及維他命。我們逐一講解：每種藥的用途、甚麼時候服用，有沒有重複。

Most useful if you see more than one doctor, or just came home from the hospital.如果你看幾位醫生，或剛出院，特別有用。

醫療保險諮詢Medicare & Medicaid help

Medicare Part D and Medicaid, explained in your language.Medicare D部分及白卡（Medicaid），用你的語言解釋。

Drug plans change every year. We can sit with you, look up your medicines in Medicare’s Plan Finder, and show what each plan would cost you.藥物計劃每年都會改變。我們可以和你一起，在 Medicare 的計劃查詢工具中輸入你的藥物，看看每個計劃要付多少錢。

We can also explain what Medicaid covers at the pharmacy counter.我們也可以解釋白卡（Medicaid）在藥房包括甚麼。

We don’t sell insurance and we can’t enroll you. We explain, you choose.我們不賣保險，也不能代你登記。我們負責解釋，由你決定。
