This month at the counter

Free screenings, vaccine days & check‑ins

  • Screening Saturday
  • Vaccine walk‑in
  • Pharmacist check‑in
  • Medicare plan review

    1. Screening Saturday

      Second Saturday of every month · 10 am–2 pm

      Free blood‑pressure, blood‑glucose and BMI screenings with our pharmacists. No appointment and no insurance needed. You leave with your numbers written down to show your doctor.

    2. Vaccine walk‑in days

      Every Tuesday & Thursday · 10 am–7 pm

      Flu, COVID‑19, shingles, pneumonia and Tdap. Bring your insurance card; we’ll check eligibility and coverage first. Other days, call and we’ll find you a time.

    3. Diabetes & blood‑pressure check‑in

      First Wednesday of every month · 3–6 pm

      Fifteen minutes with a pharmacist in our private room to go over your meter, your supplies and your questions.

    4. Medicare plan review

      Mondays, 2–6 pm, during Open Enrollment (Oct 15–Dec 7)

      Bring your Medicare card and your medicine list. We’ll help you compare Part D plans for the coming year.

    Diabetes & blood pressure

    Supplies, set‑up and a standing check‑in

    Living with diabetes or high blood pressure means a lot of small things to keep track of. We keep the supplies stocked, and we make time to go through them with you.

    1. The right supplies

      Meters, strips, lancets, pen needles and home blood‑pressure monitors, billed to your plan where it covers them.

    2. Set up at the counter

      We’ll pair a new meter, check the cuff fits, and show you how to log readings for your doctor.

    3. Check in monthly

      First Wednesday, 3–6 pm, or any time you call ahead. Free, fifteen minutes, in a private room.

    What to bring to a check‑in

    • Your meter or blood‑pressure monitor
    • Your readings log, or the app on your phone
    • Your medicines, in a bag, as you take them

    Medicaid & Medicare

    Coverage, explained across the counter

    • NY Medicaid, including managed‑care plans
    • Most Medicare Part D and Medicare Advantage drug plans
    • Most major commercial and union plans

    Bring your card. If something isn’t covered, we’ll call the plan or your doctor about an alternative before you pay anything.

    Open Enrollment runs Oct 15–Dec 7. Plan reviews Mondays 2–6 pm.

    1938

    Same corner, same counter

    The pharmacy opened on Lenox Avenue in 1938, two blocks from the park. A few families have run it since. The terrazzo floor and the brass rail are still the ones customers lean on today; the computers behind the counter are new.
