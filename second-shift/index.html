Two shifts

01

First shift

7:00 am – 5:00 pm

Bright, a bit loud, smells like toast. Wi-Fi is fast and the outlets run along every table. Stay as long as you like; we just ask for a new order every couple of hours.

  • Laptops welcome
  • Calls in the phone booth, please
  • Dogs on the patio

02

Second shift

5:00 pm – midnight

At five the overheads go off, the amber sconces come on, and the laptops go into a cubby by the door (we’ll mind it). It’s a bar now. Conversation is the only screen.

  • Laptops away
  • Walk-ins only
  • 21+ after 8 pm
