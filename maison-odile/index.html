Les petits mots

A pastry glossary — tap a word.

  • Puff pastry: butter and dough folded into hundreds of layers. Our croissants have 27.
  • A layered mousse cake, assembled in a ring and glazed. Literally “between courses.”
  • Nuts cooked in caramel, then ground into a paste. Hazelnut is classic.
  • Pastry cream enriched with butter, whipped until light. The heart of a fraisier.
  • Whipped cream, lightly sweetened with vanilla. Named for the château, not a person.
  • Sweet, crisp shortcrust for tarts — rolled thin, baked blind.
  • Brioche studded with pink-sugared almonds. A Lyonnais specialty and Odile’s childhood breakfast.

IV.

Odile Marchand

Born above her grandmother’s bakery in Lyon’s Croix-Rousse. Trained at a pastry school in Yssingeaux, then nine years in the pastry kitchens of grand hotels in Paris and Geneva, where she ran the dessert service for 400 covers a night.

She came to New York for a year in 2015. Then she met the Upper West Side, and its very particular opinions about croissants.

« Une pâtisserie, c’est de la précision au service de la tendresse. »Pastry is precision in the service of tenderness.
