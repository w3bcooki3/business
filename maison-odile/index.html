IV.

Odile Marchand

Born above her grandmother’s bakery in Lyon’s Croix-Rousse. Trained at a pastry school in Yssingeaux, then nine years in the pastry kitchens of grand hotels in Paris and Geneva, where she ran the dessert service for 400 covers a night.

She came to New York for a year in 2015. Then she met the Upper West Side, and its very particular opinions about croissants.

« Une pâtisserie, c’est de la précision au service de la tendresse. »

Pastry is precision in the service of tenderness.
