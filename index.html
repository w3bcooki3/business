What every site includes

  1. Designed for the phone firstEach site has its own phone layout, with a bottom bar for Call, Directions and the main action, and a full-screen menu.
  2. Live open or closedHours are worked out in New York time, so “Open now, until 6 pm” is always right, and today’s row is highlighted.
  3. One tap to actCall, text, WhatsApp, email or directions. No forms that go nowhere, no app to download.
  4. The details owners care aboutFor pharmacies: refills, transfers, insurance, vaccines, delivery and the pharmacists’ names. For food: what’s fresh today and what it costs.
  5. Accessible and search-readyReadable type, keyboard and screen-reader support, and business details Google can read.
  6. Fast and cheap to hostPlain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, no platform fees. It runs on GitHub Pages or any web host.
