A family bakery whose real story is the morning: what came out of the oven, what's left, and what's gone. Two directions: a practical daily bake sheet, or a bold folk-art poster.

    How these are built

    Fast, simple, yours

    • 01Phone first

      Most customers find you on a phone, standing on the sidewalk. Every page is tested on small screens from 320px up.

    • 02Real details

      Hours that know when you're open, tap-to-call, directions and menus that are easy to update.

    • 03No platform lock-in

      Plain, fast files you own. Host them almost anywhere, often for free, with no monthly builder fees.

    • 04Findable

      Clean structure, accessible markup and local-business details that help Google and Maps understand you.
