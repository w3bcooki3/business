I

The room

A former textile showroom with cast-iron columns, oak floors and north light. Three restored 1950s chairs, set far enough apart that you won’t hear the next conversation.

Espresso from a lever machine, or a pour of Japanese whisky after five. A wall of records, chosen by whoever’s in the first chair. Your coat is hung; your phone, if you like, rests in a walnut tray until we’re done.

III

Your visit

  1. 00:00

    Arrival

    Take the elevator to four. Coat hung, espresso or whisky poured. No paperwork.

  2. 00:05

    Consultation

    Ten minutes, seated, mirror turned away. How you wear it, how it grows, how much time you give it in the morning.

  3. 00:15

    The work

    Shampoo, then the cut. Elliot narrates only if you want him to.

  4. 00:50

    Finish

    Hot towel, styled with the product we’ll recommend — or none at all.

  5. 01:00

    Next time

    We note everything, so the next appointment starts where this one ended.

Notes

Cancellations

We ask for 24 hours’ notice. Within 24 hours, we charge half the service; missed appointments are charged in full. Life happens — tell us and we’ll be reasonable.

Arriving late

Your hour is your hour. If you’re fifteen minutes late, we’ll still give you our full attention, but we finish on time for the next guest.

Children

Young gentlemen aged ten and over are welcome with a parent, for the Signature Cut at $85.

Gifts

Gift cards for any service, written by hand and posted in a letterpress envelope. Email us.

Accessibility

The building has an elevator, and the studio is step-free. One chair adjusts low for guests who use a wheelchair.
