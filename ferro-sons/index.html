    Three generations

    Sixty-two years on 18th Avenue

    1. 1964

      Salvatore Ferro, a barber from Bari, opens two chairs next to a pork store. Haircuts are $1.25. The Italian radio station plays all day.

    2. 1974

      Sal Jr. starts sweeping up at fourteen. He gets his first customer at sixteen — his uncle, who doesn’t complain, which is how he knows it went well.

    3. 1989

      The shop expands to four chairs. The marble counter and the hex tile floor go in. They’re still here; so is the radio.

    4. 2011

      Grandson Vinny joins, brings clippers, fades and the first credit card machine. Sal still keeps a cash box “just in case.”

    5. Today

      Second, third and fourth generations of the same families sit in the same chairs. Sal still asks about your mother.
