    Getting equipment through insurance

    Medicare Part B, NY Medicaid and many plans cover walkers, wheelchairs, CPAP supplies, diabetic shoes and more with your doctor’s prescription.

    1. Get a prescription. Your doctor writes an order for the item, for example a walker or CPAP supplies.
    2. We check your coverage. We tell you exactly what you’ll pay before anything is ordered.
    3. Pick up or have it delivered. Get fitted in store, or we deliver to your home on the North Shore. Hospital beds and lift chairs are delivered and set up.

    Questions we hear a lot

    Can’t find your answer? Call (718) 555-0148.

    Do you deliver?

    Yes, free, across the North Shore of Staten Island, from St. George and Tompkinsville to Port Richmond and Mariners Harbor. Call to set up delivery or to ask if we reach your street.

    Where do I park, and how does the drive-up window work?

    Our free customer lot is beside the store, entered from Forest Ave just west of the building. The drive-up window is on the lot side; pull up, give your name and we bring your order to the window. It’s open all store hours.

    Can I transfer my prescriptions from another pharmacy?

    Yes. Call us with your old pharmacy’s name and the medicines you take. We contact them and your insurance and call you when your first fill is ready.

    Do you take my insurance?

    We take most plans, including Medicare, NY Medicaid, Fidelis Care, Healthfirst and EmblemHealth. See the insurance list, or call and we’ll check your plan.

    Will insurance pay for my walker, CPAP supplies or diabetic shoes?

    Often, yes, with a prescription from your doctor. Medicare Part B, Medicaid and many plans cover medical equipment. We check your coverage and tell you what you’ll pay before we order.

    Do I need an appointment for a flu shot?

    No. Walk in any time we’re open and bring your insurance card. Ask which ages we can vaccinate.

    How long does a new prescription take?

    Most are ready the same day, often within 30 minutes. If we need to order a medicine or get insurance approval, we’ll tell you when it will be ready.

    Can you put my medicines in pill packs?

    Yes, free. We sort your medicines by day and time into blister packs each month. Ask a pharmacist to get started.
