Keeping an eye on your numbers

  1. Get a reading. Free at the counter, written on your log card.
  2. Track at home. We help you choose a monitor with the right cuff size and show you how to take an accurate reading.
  3. Check in with us. Bring your log after a week or two. We go over it with your medicines and share it with your doctor if you ask.

Questions we hear a lot

Something else? Call (718) 555-0142.

Do you deliver?

Yes, free, across Flatbush, East Flatbush, Ditmas Park and Prospect Lefferts Gardens. Ask for delivery when you call or text your refill.

Can I transfer my prescriptions to you?

Yes. Call us with your old pharmacy’s name and location. We contact them and move your prescriptions over, so you don’t need to call them.

Do you take my insurance?

We take most plans, including Medicare Part D, NY Medicaid (NYRx), Fidelis Care, Healthfirst, EmblemHealth and MetroPlusHealth. See the full list or call us to check.

Do I need an appointment for a flu shot?

No. Walk in any time we’re open and bring your insurance card. For a travel consult, call or text ahead to book a time.

How long does a new prescription take?

Most are ready in about 15–30 minutes. If we need to order a medicine or check with your insurance, we’ll tell you when it will be ready.

Can I take sea moss or bush tea with my blood-pressure medicine?

Ask us first. Some herbs and supplements change how medicines work. Bring the product or a photo of the label and a pharmacist will check, free.

Can you put my medicines in pill packs?

Yes. We sort your medicines into packs by day and time. Ask the pharmacist if pill packs are right for you.
