    Since 1958

    Three generations. One kettle.

    1. 1958

      Abe Feldman, a baker from Białystok by way of the Bronx, rents a storefront on Grand Street. Bagels are three cents. He sleeps on the flour sacks the first winter.

    2. 1987

      His son Lenny takes over, adds the schmear counter, and refuses — loudly — to make blueberry bagels. We still don’t.

    3. 2019

      Lenny’s daughters Rachel and Miriam come home: Rachel from a restaurant kitchen in Chicago, Miriam from law school (“I’m still arguing, just about dough”). They add babka, a proper rye, and the name on the awning.

    4. Today

      The kettle is from 1971. The cutting board is from 1958. The line on Sunday goes past Suffolk Street, and moves fast.
