Custom-made medicines, explained simply

Pharmacists have always made medicines by hand. We still do, for the times the standard version from the factory doesn’t fit.

  1. 1

    Your doctor or vet writes it

    They send the prescription to us, or you bring it in. If something needs adjusting, we call them, so you don’t have to.

  2. 2

    We tell you the price

    Before we make anything, you get a clear price. Insurance sometimes covers it; often it doesn’t, and you’ll know which.

  3. 3

    We make it here, in 1–2 days

    Measured and mixed by our pharmacists in the back of the shop, labeled by hand, then picked up or delivered free.

  • A liquid instead of a pill
  • Strawberry flavor for a 4-year-old
  • Dye-free or gluten-free
  • A smaller dose than the factory makes
  • A tuna-flavored treat for your cat
  • A cream mixed for sensitive skin

How fast is a regular refill?

Most are ready in about 15 minutes if you walk in, or by the time we text you back. If we need to call your doctor for a new prescription, we’ll tell you how long it will take.

Is delivery really free?

Yes, for every order in Cobble Hill, Carroll Gardens, Boerum Hill, Brooklyn Heights, Gowanus and Park Slope north of 9th Street. No minimum. Order by 4pm weekdays or 2pm Saturday for same-day.

Do I need an appointment for a flu shot?

No. Walk in any time we’re open. Kids 3 and up can get flu and COVID shots with a parent or guardian. Bring your insurance card if you have it.

Can you make a medicine my child will actually take?

Usually, yes. We add flavor to most liquid medicines for free. If a medicine only comes as a pill, we can often make it as a flavored liquid. Ask at the counter or call.

My vet gave me a prescription. Can you fill it?

Yes. Your vet can send it to us electronically or by fax, or you can bring the paper. We fill many pet medicines and can make chews or flavored liquids.

What if my insurance isn’t on your list?

Call us. Many plans run through a pharmacy benefit manager we already work with, and we can usually check in about two minutes. If it’s not covered, ask for our cash price.
