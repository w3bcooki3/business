What’s usually covered, and what isn’t

Every plan is a little different. Here’s what we see most often.

Usually covered

  • Most prescriptions from your doctor
  • Flu, COVID, RSV, shingles, pneumonia and Tdap vaccines, often $0
  • Breast pumps during pregnancy
  • Diabetes supplies: meters, strips, lancets
  • Blister packs and med sync (no charge from us)

Often not covered

  • Custom-made medicines (sometimes covered, often not)
  • Pet prescriptions
  • Vitamins, skincare and most front-store items
  • Some brand-name drugs when a generic exists

For anything not covered, you get a clear price before we fill or make it. FSA and HSA cards work for most medical items.

Insurance questions

Grace handles most of these. Ask for her.

My plan isn’t on the list. Can I still use Verbena?

Probably. Many plans are run by a pharmacy benefit manager such as Express Scripts, CVS Caremark or OptumRx, which we accept. Call us with your card and we’ll check.

Why did my copay change?

Plans change prices each January, and your deductible resets. We’ll explain what happened and look for a cheaper option, like a generic or a 90-day supply.

My insurance needs a “prior authorization.” What now?

That means your plan wants your doctor to explain why you need the medicine. We send the request to your doctor and follow up until it’s sorted. We’ll text you along the way.

Do you take Medicaid and Child Health Plus?

Yes, both, including Medicaid managed care through Fidelis Care, Healthfirst, MetroPlus and EmblemHealth.

Will insurance pay for my pet’s medicine?

Human insurance doesn’t cover pets. Some pet insurance reimburses you afterward; we’ll give you a receipt to send in.
