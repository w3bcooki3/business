A family pharmacy, on a family street

Verbena opened on Court Street in 2014, in a ground-floor shop that had been a pharmacy for decades before. We wanted to keep it one.

Our story

Why “Verbena”? It was the first plant in the flower boxes outside, and it’s still there every summer.

Dr. Hana Okafor-Lind spent ten years as a hospital pharmacist before opening Verbena. She missed knowing her patients, so she opened the kind of pharmacy she grew up with: small, close by, and run by people who pick up the phone.

  1. 2014

    We open at 214 Court StreetHana and one technician, a bike for deliveries, and flower boxes out front.

  2. 2016

    Teo joinsBorn and raised in Carroll Gardens, Teo starts our walk-in vaccine clinic.

  3. 2018

    Custom-made medicinesWe build a small mixing room in the back, mostly for kids and pets.
