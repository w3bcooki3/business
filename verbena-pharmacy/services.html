Prescriptions & refills

The everyday part, done without the waiting. Call (718) 555-0116 or text (718) 555-0118.

New prescriptions

Ask your doctor to send it to Verbena Pharmacy, 214 Court Street. Most are ready in 15 minutes once it arrives. We text you when it’s ready.

Refills

Call, text a photo of the label, or walk in. No app or account. We can also text you when a refill is due.

Free transfers

Tell us where your prescriptions are now. We call the old pharmacy and move everything, usually the same day.

Med sync

Taking several medicines? We line up all your refills so they’re ready on one day each month. One trip, one delivery.

Blister packs

Weekly packs sorted by day and time of day, so it’s easy to see whether a dose was taken. Free for anyone on med sync. Helpful for parents and grandparents.

Medicine check-ins

Bring every bottle in a bag and we’ll go through them with you: what each is for, what to stop, what doesn’t mix.

Walk-in vaccines

No appointment. Come in any time we’re open, and we’ll usually have you in and out in 15 minutes. Most vaccines are $0 with insurance, Medicare or Medicaid.

Kids 3 and up

Children 3 to 17 can get flu and COVID shots here with a parent or guardian. We go slowly, and there are stickers.

What to bring

Your insurance or Medicare card, and a photo ID for adults. No card? Ask about free and low-cost options.

Your record

We send every shot to the New York State immunization registry, so your doctor sees it too.

Custom-made medicines

For when the standard version doesn’t work: a liquid instead of a pill, a better flavor for kids, a dye-free version, the right dose for your cat.

Some people call this compounding. It simply means a pharmacist makes your medicine by hand, to your doctor’s instructions, instead of a factory making the same thing for everyone. Dr. Hana and Teo make everything in the back of our shop on Court Street.

  • Ready in 1–2 days for most medicines
  • Clear price before we start. Often $25–$60; insurance sometimes pays, often doesn’t
  • We talk to your doctor or vet about strength, flavor and form
  • Free delivery in our usual neighborhoods

We make medicines for people and pets. We don’t make sterile injectables; we’ll point you to a pharmacy that does.

Families & baby care

Bring the stroller in, there’s room. We’re used to sleepy parents with a lot of questions.

Kids’ medicine flavoring

Free on any liquid medicine. Grape, cherry, strawberry, bubblegum, watermelon or banana. Your child picks.

Dosing help

We’ll work out the right dose by weight for fever reducers and allergy medicine, and mark the syringe for you.

Breast pumps through insurance

Most plans cover a pump during pregnancy. We check your plan, order it and handle the paperwork.

New-baby basics

Baby thermometers, vitamin D drops, saline and nasal aspirators, diaper cream, and gentle baby wash.

Shots for the family

Flu and COVID for kids 3 and up, Tdap and RSV during pregnancy, and everything grown-ups need.

Questions, any time

Call and ask for a pharmacist. “Is this rash normal?” and “Can I take this while nursing?” are good questions.

Pet prescriptions

Your vet writes it, we fill it. Pick it up with your own medicines, or we’ll deliver it with a treat.

  • Vets can e-prescribe or fax (718) 555-0117
  • Many common pet medicines cost less here than at the vet
  • Flavored liquids (chicken, beef, tuna) and soft chews
  • Doses made to your pet’s weight, even for very small cats
  • Pet prescriptions are usually not covered by insurance; we tell you the price first
