Privacy notice

How we protect your health information, in plain language. Effective January 1, 2026.

The short version

  • We keep your prescriptions and health information private, as federal law (HIPAA) and New York law require.
  • We share it only to fill your prescriptions, get paid by your insurance, or when the law requires it.
  • We never sell your information or use it for advertising.
  • This website has no tracking, no cookies and no forms. Nothing you type here is sent to us.

What we collect at the pharmacy

Your name, date of birth, address, phone number, insurance details, prescriptions, allergies and the medicines you take. We need this to fill prescriptions safely.

How we use and share it

  • Treatment: talking with your doctor, vet or other pharmacies about your medicines.
  • Payment: sending claims to your insurance plan.
  • Operations: refill reminders by text or phone, and quality checks.
  • Required by law: the New York State immunization registry, and public health or legal requests.

For anything else, we ask your written permission first. You can take it back at any time.

Your rights

  • Get a copy of your pharmacy records.
  • Ask us to correct something that’s wrong.
  • Ask us to contact you a certain way, like texts only.
  • Get a list of who we’ve shared your information with.
  • Get a paper copy of this notice at the counter.

Texts and delivery

When you text us, we reply only about your prescriptions. Delivery bags are sealed and labeled with your name and address only. Reply STOP to end text reminders.

This website

This site is a set of plain pages. It doesn’t use cookies, analytics or third-party tracking, and it has no forms. The insurance checker runs only in your browser; what you type isn’t saved or sent anywhere.

Questions or concerns

Ask for Dr. Hana Okafor-Lind, our privacy officer, at (718) 555-0116 or at the counter. You can also file a complaint with the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services, Office for Civil Rights. We will never treat you differently for filing a complaint.
