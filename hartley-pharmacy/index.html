Switching from a chain pharmacy?

Transfers are free and take about a day. You don’t need to call your old pharmacy, or your doctor.

  1. Tell us where your prescriptions are nowCall, text or stop in with the pharmacy name and location, and the medicines you take.

Questions neighbors ask

How long does a new prescription take?

Most are ready in under an hour. If your plan needs a prior authorization or we need to order the medicine, we’ll call or text you with a time, usually next morning.

Can someone else pick up my prescription?

Yes. Let us know their name and they can pick up with their own ID. For some controlled medicines we’ll ask for a quick call from you.

Do I need an appointment for a flu shot?

No. Walk in any time we’re open. If there’s a short wait, we’ll text you when it’s your turn so you can keep shopping on Bleecker.

My insurance isn’t on your list. Can I still fill here?

Call us. Plans change names often, and we can usually check yours in two minutes. If it isn’t covered, we’ll give you a cash price and compare it with discount cards.

Can you talk to my mom’s doctor, or to me about her medicines?

Yes, with her written OK. Nora sets up a simple permission form so we can speak with family members and caregivers, wherever they live.

Do you sell medicines without a prescription?

Of course. Our front store carries cold and flu, allergy, pain relief, vitamins and first aid, and a pharmacist is always there to help you pick.

If it’s an emergency, don’t wait for us

Call 911. For a possible poisoning or overdose, call Poison Control at 1-800-222-1222, open 24 hours. For a mental health crisis, call or text 988.
