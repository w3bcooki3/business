Medicare open enrollment help

Every fall, Part D and Medicare Advantage plans change their prices and the drugs they cover. A plan that was right last year can cost you hundreds more this year.

Bring your medicine list (or just let us print it), and a pharmacist will compare plans with you for free. We’ll show you what each one would cost for the medicines you actually take. We don’t sell plans and don’t get paid by any of them.

What to bring

  • Your red, white and blue Medicare card
  • Your current plan cardAnd the “Annual Notice of Change” letter, if you got one.
  • Your doctors’ namesSo we can check they’re in network for Advantage plans.
  • Your income, if it’s limitedYou may qualify for Extra Help or EPIC, New York’s senior drug program. We’ll help you apply.

Prior authorization: we fight the paperwork

“Prior authorization required” means your plan wants your doctor to explain why you need a medicine before they’ll pay. It’s a hassle. We take it on.

“Sam once spent three weeks on one inhaler. He won.”

Dolores, pharmacy technician
  1. We tell you right awayIf your plan rejects a prescription, we explain why in plain words, before you leave the counter.
  2. We contact your doctor’s officeWe send them the plan’s form and the details they need, and ask if a covered alternative would work.
  3. We follow upEvery day or two, with the doctor and the plan, until there’s an answer. Most take 2–5 business days.
  4. You don’t go withoutWhile we wait, we can often give a few days’ supply, or find a fair cash price.
  5. If it’s denied, we help you appealWe’ll help your doctor file an appeal and tell you your options.

Insurance questions

My plan isn’t on the list. What now?

Call (212) 555-0142. Many plans use a pharmacy benefit manager we work with, so we may take it even if the name on your card isn’t listed. If we don’t, we’ll give you a cash price.

Do you take Medicaid managed care plans?

Yes. Since 2023, New York Medicaid pharmacy benefits go through NYRx, and we accept it, along with Fidelis Care, Healthfirst, MetroPlus and other plans for their non-pharmacy benefits like supplies.

What is EPIC?

EPIC is New York State’s prescription program for people 65 and older. It works alongside Medicare Part D to lower your copays. We can help you sign up.

Can I use a discount card instead of my insurance?

Yes, for any prescription. Sometimes it’s cheaper than your copay. Just know the cost won’t count toward your plan’s deductible.

My plan wants me to use mail order. Can I still fill here?

Often yes. Many plans allow 90-day fills at independent pharmacies. We’ll check, and if you’re required to use mail order, we’ll still fill a short supply to tide you over.

How do I pay for vaccines?

Most vaccines are $0 with insurance, including Medicare and Medicaid. Self-pay prices are on our services page.
