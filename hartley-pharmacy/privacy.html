Privacy

Privacy notice

Last updated October 1, 2026. Your health information stays between you, us, and the people you choose.

Your health information

As a pharmacy, we follow HIPAA and New York State law. We use your prescription and health information only to fill your prescriptions, give vaccines, bill your insurance, and coordinate with your doctors. We never sell it.

We share it with family members or caregivers only when you’ve said yes, either in writing or, for a quick question, in person. You can change who we talk to at any time.

You have the right to see and get a copy of your pharmacy records, ask us to correct them, ask for a list of who we’ve shared them with, and get our full Notice of Privacy Practices. Ask at the counter, or call (212) 555-0142.

This website

This website doesn’t use cookies, analytics or tracking scripts, and it has no forms. The insurance search runs entirely in your browser: what you type is never sent to us or anyone else.

When you tap a phone number, text link or map link, your phone or browser opens that app, and that company’s own privacy policy applies.

Text messages

If you text refills to (212) 555-0143, we keep those messages only as long as needed to fill your order and as the law requires. Please don’t text anything you’d prefer to say in person. Reply STOP at any time to stop refill reminders.

Questions or concerns

Ask for Ruth Hartley-Diaz, our privacy officer, at the pharmacy, call (212) 555-0142, or email privacy@hartleypharmacy.example. You can also contact the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services Office for Civil Rights. We will never treat you differently for raising a concern.
