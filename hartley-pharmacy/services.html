Senior & caregiver care

A lot of our neighbors have been coming here for forty years. As their medicine lists get longer, we make it simpler. And if you’re the son in Philadelphia or the daughter in Ohio, we’ll talk to you too, with your parent’s OK.

  1. Start with a medication reviewNora sits down with you (or on the phone with your family) and goes through every medicine, vitamin and supplement.
  2. We sync your refillsEverything is lined up to one day a month, so there’s one pickup or one delivery. No more running out of one pill mid-month.
  3. Weekly blister packs, if they helpEach dose sealed in its own bubble, labeled by day and time: morning, noon, evening, bedtime. Free with med sync.
  4. Delivered and checked onWe bring the packs to the door, and call if a doctor changes a dose or a refill is running low.
  5. Family stays in the loopWith a signed permission form, we’ll call or text a caregiver after each delivery or change.
