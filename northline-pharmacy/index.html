Questions we hear a lot

Something else? Text (212) 555-0164 and a pharmacist will write back.

Can you transfer my prescriptions from CVS, Walgreens or Duane Reade?

Yes, and it’s free. Tell us your old pharmacy and what you take, and we call them and move everything over, usually the same day. Controlled medicines may need a new prescription from your doctor; we’ll let you know.

How fast is a refill?

Most refills are ready in about 15 minutes. New prescriptions that need an insurance approval can take longer; we’ll text you when it’s ready.

Is delivery really free?

Yes. Same-day delivery is free from 72nd to 125th Street, Hudson River to Central Park, for orders in by 4pm on weekdays and 2pm on Saturday. No minimum.

Do I need an appointment for a flu shot?

No. Walk in any time we’re open. Kids 3 and up can get flu and COVID shots here. Bring your insurance card.

Can I get birth control here without seeing a doctor first?

In New York, pharmacists can now dispense certain self-administered hormonal birth control (like the pill, patch or ring) after a short screening. Ask Dr. Nair; she’ll go through a few health questions with you in our private room and tell you whether it’s a good fit or whether you should see a doctor.

What if my insurance says no?

Rosa will call your plan, look for a covered alternative, or ask your doctor for a prior authorization. If you pay cash, we’ll check discount cards to find the lowest price.

Do you have a private space to talk?

Yes. We have a small consult room for vaccines, PrEP and any conversation you’d rather not have at the counter.
