Privacy notice

The short version: your health information stays between you, us and the people involved in your care.

Last updated October 1, 2026.

This website

This site doesn’t have forms, accounts or logins, and it doesn’t use advertising trackers. The insurance checker runs entirely in your browser; nothing you type is sent to us or anyone else.

When you tap “Call” or “Text,” your phone opens its own app. Messages you send us by text are stored on our pharmacy system so we can fill your refill.

Your health information

As a pharmacy, we follow HIPAA and New York State law. We use your information to fill prescriptions, bill your insurance, check for drug interactions, and contact you about your care. We don’t sell it, ever.

  • We share information with your insurance plan and your prescribers when needed for your care or payment.
  • We report vaccines to the New York State Immunization Information System, as the law requires.
  • Someone you name can pick up for you. Just tell us who.
  • You can ask for a copy of your pharmacy records, or ask us to correct something.

Texting us

Regular text messages aren’t encrypted. Please send only what we need for a refill: your name, date of birth and the medicine or Rx number. For anything sensitive, call or come in and use our private room.

Questions or complaints

Ask for Dr. Kevin Oduya, our privacy officer, at (212) 555-0163 or privacy@northlinerx.com. You can also contact the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services Office for Civil Rights. We will never hold it against you.

A printed copy of our full Notice of Privacy Practices is available at the counter.

In an emergency, don’t wait for us

  • 911 for a medical emergency
  • 1-800-222-1222 Poison Control, 24/7
  • 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, call or text
