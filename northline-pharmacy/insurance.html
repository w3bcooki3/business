Medicare open enrollment help

Every fall, Part D plans change their prices and covered drugs. A plan that worked this year may cost you hundreds more next year.

Bring your medicine list (or let us print it), and Dr. Oduya or Sam will sit down with you and compare plans on Medicare’s Plan Finder. It’s free, takes about 30 minutes, and we don’t sell plans, so there’s no pitch.

Insurance questions

Rosa has heard them all. Text (212) 555-0164 if yours isn’t here.

Do you take Medicare Part B for diabetes supplies?

Yes. Meters, strips and lancets are covered under Part B, and many CGMs are too. Bring your red-white-and-blue Medicare card along with your Part D card.

My plan says I have to use mail order. Can I still use you?

Often, yes. Many plans let you get 90-day fills at a local pharmacy for the same copay. Rosa will check yours.

What is EPIC?

EPIC is New York State’s prescription program for people 65 and older. It works alongside Medicare Part D to lower your copays. We can help you apply.

I have a Columbia or Mount Sinai staff plan. Do you take it?

Most hospital and university staff plans run through a pharmacy benefit manager such as Express Scripts, CVS Caremark or OptumRx, all of which we take. Look for the logo on the back of your card, or just bring it in.

What if my medicine needs a prior authorization?

We’ll send the request to your doctor and follow up until it’s approved. We text you as soon as it goes through.

Do you accept discount cards like GoodRx?

Yes. If you pay cash, we’ll compare our price with discount cards and charge you whichever is lower.
