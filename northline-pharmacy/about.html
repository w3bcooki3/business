How Northline started

Kevin Oduya grew up in Nairobi and moved to the Upper West Side for pharmacy school. After eight years at a big chain, he got tired of telling people to “wait 45 minutes” and never seeing the same face twice.

In 2009 he opened Northline on Amsterdam at 96th, with one counter, a chalkboard out front and a promise: you’ll talk to a pharmacist who knows you. The name comes from the 1 train, the line that brought him uptown.

Seventeen years on, the chalkboard is still out every morning. We know which regulars want their refills texted and which want a call, which kids prefer grape, and which dog gets the chicken-flavored pills.

  1. 2009Dr. Kevin Oduya opens Northline in a former shoe repair shop on Amsterdam Avenue.
  2. 2012Free delivery starts, on Kevin’s own bike, from 86th to 110th.
  3. 2016Dr. Priya Nair joins, and walk-in vaccines become a year-round thing.
  4. 2020We stay open every day of the pandemic and deliver to 3,000 neighbors.
  5. 2023Pickup lockers go in for night-shift nurses and late commuters.
  6. 2026Delivery now runs 72nd to 125th. Still independent, still on the corner.
