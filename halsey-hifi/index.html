Listening nights

Album Night. Every Wednesday, 8 pm.

The system

Turned up just enough to feel it in the floorboards.

Everything plays from vinyl, through a signal path we can name at the counter. No playlists, no shuffle, no ads between tracks.

  1. 01The recordOur own crate plus whatever you brought on Saturday
  2. 02TurntableBelt‑drive deck on an isolation shelf
  3. 03Tube phono stageWarm, quiet, a little glow behind the bar
  4. 04ReceiverA restored 1970s silver‑face, about 60 watts a side
  5. 05Horn speakersA pair of big walnut cabinets, aimed at the couch
  6. 06YouWindow seat, couch, or the bar — the sweet spot moves
  • i. Requests go on paper, at the counter. We play them in the next set.
  • ii. The volume rises after 5 pm. Mornings stay quiet enough to read.
  • iii. Every record we play is in the bin by the door — ask, and we’ll show you the sleeve.
