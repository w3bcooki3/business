    03

    How a morning goes

    We press in small batches on a hydraulic press, the slow way. It takes about three pounds of produce to fill one green-juice bottle.

    1. 4:45Produce in

      Delivered from the Brooklyn Terminal Market and two farms upstate.

    2. 5:00Wash & chop

      Everything scrubbed. Nothing peeled that doesn’t need it.

    3. 5:30Press

      Ground into pulp, wrapped in cloth, pressed under 20 tons.

    4. 6:45Bottle & label

      Each label is dated by hand.

    5. 7:00Doors open

      The pulp goes to a community garden on North 7th.

    We don’t sell cleanses. Your liver and kidneys already do that job. Drink juice because it tastes good, alongside real meals.
