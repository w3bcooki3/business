Service map

Eight lines. One counter that knows your name.

Every service in the store has its own code and colour — on this site and on the signs inside. Follow the line, find the counter.

  1. Rx

    Counter 1 · front of store

    Prescriptions & refills

    New prescriptions from any doctor, e-prescribed or on paper. We text you when it’s ready, and refills can be synced to one pickup day a month.

    • Most major plans
    • Medicaid & Medicare Part D
    • Text-when-ready
    Text a refill
  2. Tx

    Phone or Counter 1

    Transfers

    Switching is one call. Tell us your current pharmacy and what you take; we contact them and move everything over. You don’t have to call them.

    • From chains or independents
    • All your refills at once
    Call to switch
  3. Vx

    Counter 3 · back right, private room

    Walk-in vaccines

    No appointment for flu shots. Other vaccines — COVID-19, shingles, pneumonia, Tdap — depend on season and supply, so call ahead for those. Last walk-in 30 minutes before close.

    • No appointment
    • Bring insurance card & ID
    Check today’s stock
  4. Dv

    Astoria · 11102 11103 11105 11106

    Free delivery

    Free same-day delivery anywhere in Astoria for orders in by 4 PM, Monday to Saturday. We text a delivery window; someone needs to be home to sign.

    • Free in Astoria
    • Order by 4 PM
    • Mon–Sat
    Text for delivery
  5. BP

    Station by the front window

    Blood-pressure checks

    Free, walk-in, about five minutes, any time we’re open. A pharmacist can go over the numbers with you; it’s a screening, so share your readings with your doctor.

    • Free
    • Walk in
    See today’s hours
  6. Mc

    Consult desk · by appointment or walk-in

    Medicare & cost help

    Ask for a price check: we compare your plan, generics and manufacturer savings programs. During Medicare Open Enrollment (Oct 15 – Dec 7) we’ll sit down and review your Part D plan with you, free.

    • Free plan review
    • Price checks
    • Savings programs
    Book a review
  7. Pk

    Set up by phone · pick up or delivered

    Pill packs

    Your daily medicines sorted into tear-off packets, labelled by date and time of day. Refills synced, one delivery a month, no extra charge.

    • No extra charge
    • Synced refills
    Call to set up
  8. Eq

    Aisle 6 · back left

    Medical equipment

    Canes, walkers, wheelchairs, compression, bathroom safety and diabetic supplies. With a prescription, many items can be billed to Medicare Part B. Ask us.

    • In stock or next day
    • Medicare Part B billing
    Ask what’s in stock
